\section{Level 1 --- Real DotBot Hardware}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Where L0 established the \emph{algorithmic} ceiling on abstract grids, Level~1
measures the \emph{physical} limit on real DotBots, where the threshold between the
robot's turning footprint and the cell size actually bites. The same PIBT planner
drives the hardware through a step-by-step barrier (100\,mm arrival threshold, 8\,s
per-step timeout): the planner advances one synchronous step, the bots are commanded
to their next cells, and the loop waits until every bot is within threshold of its
target before re-synchronising from the LH2 localisation. The headline quantity is the
\textbf{plan-to-real gap} $\Delta = \text{success}_{\text{L0}} - \text{success}_{\text{L1}}$,
which isolates how much of any degradation comes from hardware rather than from the
algorithm.

The batch harness (\texttt{test\_real\_dotbot\_pibt\_*bots\_*runs.py}, with the shared
logger \texttt{l1\_metrics.py}) records one row per run to \texttt{l1\_per\_run.csv} and
the per-$(\text{grid}, N)$ aggregate to \texttt{l1\_summary.csv}, using columns aligned
with the L0 results CSV so the two levels join directly.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Metrics}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Success rate} --- bots reaching their goal cell over $N$, averaged
          over seeds (mean $\pm$ 95\,\% CI).
    \item \textbf{Overshoot} --- real stop distance to the target versus the 100\,mm
          threshold; the direct measurement of the turning limit.
    \item \textbf{Step-timeout rate} --- fraction of (bot $\times$ step) that did not
          settle within the 8\,s barrier.
    \item \textbf{Throughput} --- goals completed per minute (the intralogistics framing).
\end{itemize}
